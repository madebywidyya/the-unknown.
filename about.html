ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

THE UNKNOWN

A fictional interactive mystery investigation experience designed for curiosity, careful observation, and critical thinking.

Players take the role of an independent investigator exploring a mysterious digital archive. Cases, evidence, challenges, and locations are entirely fictional and created for entertainment and practice in logical reasoning.

Progress is saved automatically in your browser. No account is required. You can reset progress at any time from the Profile page.

All stories are original, non-graphic, and suitable for teenage audiences and above.
